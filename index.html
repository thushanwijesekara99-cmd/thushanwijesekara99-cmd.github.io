03 Method Statement — How I Build with Claude
1. Understand the real problem. I start with the people who will use it: what goes wrong today, and what “done” looks like.
2. Write the spec. A full requirements document: users, rules, screens, data and testable acceptance criteria. Dirhamly’s SRS has 21 functional requirements and a worked money example.
3. Turn the spec into a staged build prompt. The spec becomes precise instructions for Claude, split into parts that each work on their own. OfficeFlow is built in nine stages, from login to backup.
4. Build and test in small steps. One part at a time with Claude, tested before the next begins, with a working copy kept after each step.
5. Make repeated work reusable. Tasks I do often become custom Claude skills, like my weekly attendance report: one request, one finished A4 Excel report.
